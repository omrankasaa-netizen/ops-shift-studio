Fair questions

What if my needs change mid-project?

Scope changes are discussed and re-quoted in writing before any extra work happens. You will never find a surprise on an invoice.

Do I really own everything?

Yes — your domain, business data, credentials and code stay under your control, with full export capability. If you ever move on, your system moves with you.

What happens after launch?

Every package includes a support period and training. After that, the optional Care retainer ($50–$150/month) keeps your system updated, backed up and monitored — including your ad pixels.

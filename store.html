The pixel: invisible code that decides
if your ads make money

A pixel is a tiny piece of code we install on your store. It tells Meta and TikTok what visitors do — what they view, what they add to cart, what they buy. Here is why that matters:

🎯
Know which ad actually sellsSee exactly which ad brought each order — not just likes and comments.
🔁
Bring visitors backPeople who browsed but did not buy see your ads again. This "retargeting" is usually the cheapest sale you will ever get.
👥
Find more buyersMeta and TikTok learn who your buyers are and automatically find more people like them.
🛑
Stop guessingWithout a pixel, ad money is a guess. With it, every dollar is measured.
Included from day one: we install and test your Meta and TikTok pixels — browser-side and server-side (Conversions API) — before you spend anything on ads. Aura and Trending Store run this exact setup today.
